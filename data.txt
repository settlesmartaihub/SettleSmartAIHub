   - Format property recommendations
   - Apply response interceptor
   - Return standardized API response
   - Track conversation progress

project idea

SettleSmart AI Intelligent Property Matching on WhatsApp. Your Smart Way Home in
Nigeria.
1. Our Mission: To make finding and securing property in Nigeria simple, trustworthy, and
accessible for everyone through intelligent conversational technology.
2. The Problem We Solve: Nigeria's property market is fraught with challenges for both
seekers and agents:
● Fragmentation & Inefficiency: Property search is scattered across informal
channels, making it time-consuming and stressful.
● Widespread Mistrust: Fake listings, duplicate properties, and unreliable agents lead
to scams and frustration.
● Digital & Accessibility Divide: Existing online portals are often complex,
data-intensive, and cater primarily to tech-savvy, affluent users, excluding a vast
majority.
● Agent Marginalization: Thousands of legitimate independent agents lack affordable
digital tools for visibility and lead management.
3. Our Solution: SettleSmart AI SettleSmart AI is a multilingual virtual assistant on
WhatsApp that revolutionizes property discovery:
● Conversational Search: Users chat with our AI in plain English or Pidgin (Yoruba,
Hausa, Igbo planned), using text or voice to describe their needs (e.g., "I need a
2-bedroom flat in Lugbe under ₦800k").
● Verified Connections: We match seekers with vetted, local real estate agents and
curated, verified property listings, complete with images and details, directly within
WhatsApp.
